3. About Arteon LLC

Building the Bridge Between Capital and Energy Innovation

Arteon LLC was established in 2023 as an independent corporate entity with a clear vision: to become a trusted bridge between strong capital and cutting-edge innovations in the energy sector. Headquartered in Sheridan, Wyoming, USA — a strategic commercial hub — we manage international activities aimed at shaping the present and future of global energy.

Our jurisdiction provides maximum stability, transparent corporate governance, and the flexibility necessary for executing complex international transactions and cross-border energy investments.

Vision and Mission

Global energy is undergoing an unprecedented transformation. Transitioning toward sustainable, resilient, and efficient energy sources requires not only financial capital but also deep technological and commercial expertise. We seek out, support, and scale projects that deliver tangible value — reducing grid dependence, optimizing operational costs, and ensuring overall grid stability.
